## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies a serene, grounded, and mindful relationship between urban dwellers and plant life. It bridges horticultural expertise with approachable, effortless micro-routines. The personality is nurturing, calm, structured, and organic—inviting the user into an oasis away from digital noise. Every interaction reinforces growth, tactile reward, and confidence in one's personal garden or indoor sanctuary.

### Target Audience
- Urban apartment gardeners, indoor plant hobbyists, balcony vegetable growers, and small-scale yard caretakers.
- Mobile-first MiniApp users prioritizing fast check-ins, watering reminders, botanical diagnostics, and visual care logs.

### Emotional Response
- **Serenity & Reassurance:** Relieving the anxiety of plant care ("Did I overwater this?", "Is there enough light?").
- **Grounded Connection:** Evoking the tactile, sensory feel of rich potting soil, dew-kissed foliage, and morning sunlight.
- **Micro-Achievement:** Celebrating quiet developmental milestones (a new leaf unfurling, a bloom emerging, a timely pruning).

### Design Style: Modern Organic & Soft Tactile
A synthesis of clean Scandinavian minimalism and warm, botanical tactility:
- **Quiet Surfaces:** Soft sage-tinted canvases, gentle borders, and warm clay highlights avoid stark digital whites.
- **Airy Hierarchy:** Generous negative space paired with clear grouping to minimize cognitive overload inside narrow web-view wrappers.
- **Organic Softness:** Generous corner radii, pill-shaped tags, and soft ambient drop shadows that emulate filtered canopy light.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from healthy foliage, damp clay pots, and sunlit mist:

### Core Tokens
- **Primary (`#1E4B38` - Deep Forest Green):** Provides authoritative grounding, structural weight, primary navigation states, and primary actions. Reflects deep, mature plant canopies.
- **Secondary (`#D97746` - Terracotta Clay):** Represents terracotta planters, dry soil cues, warmth, and attention-requiring care alerts (e.g., watering schedules overdue, fertilizing actions).
- **Tertiary (`#3E9B72` - Vital Emerald / Mint Leaf):** Highlights vibrant growth, completed tasks, health status badges, and positive progression trackers.
- **Neutral (`#232A26` - Charcoal Moss):** High-legibility text neutral with an organic undertone, avoiding pure unnatural black (`#000000`).

### Surface & Functional Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#F7F9F6` - Soft Off-White / Light Sage Tint):** Prevents eye strain during early morning or nighttime indoor gardening sessions.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Lily White):** High-clarity foreground containers, task cards, and interactive modal sheets.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#EEF3EC` - Dew Mist):** Inset wells, disabled inputs, and secondary visual dividers.
- **Accent Muted (`#F9EFE9` - Warm Sand Tint):** Background container for terracotta badges and gentle cautionary indicators.

## Typography

The typography system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, combining crisp modern geometry with soft, humanist terminals that convey friendliness and clear readability across variable-density smartphone screens.

### Principles & Execution
- **Botanical Names & Plant Varieties:** Plant species names (e.g., *Monstera deliciosa*) use `body-md` or `headline-md` rendered in regular or italicized style to maintain horticultural rigor.
- **High-Scannability Tracking:** Upper labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) use positive letter-spacing for date stamps, sensor parameters (humidity, lux, soil moisture), and status tags.
- **Compact Vertical Flow:** Line heights are configured closely to support compact MiniApp scroll areas while preventing line clashes in multi-line care notes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for **MiniApp viewports** running inside mobile host platforms (e.g., Zalo, WeChat, native iOS/Android web-containers).

### Layout System
- **Grid Structure:** A 4-column fluid mobile grid on standard phones (`<480px`), with a strict `margin` of `1rem` (16px) to maximize usable touch real estate. On foldables or small tablets (`481px - 768px`), it gracefully adapts to a 6-to-8 column structure with a maximum content container capped at `640px` centered.
- **Safe Area Insets:** Layout automatically adheres to platform native status bar headers and bottom navigation gestures (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` + 12px padding).
- **Rhythm & Hierarchy:** Vertical spacing follows an 8pt step system (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 14px, `space-lg` = 20px, `space-xl` = 28px). Dense telemetry data (temperature, hydration levels) uses `space-xs` to `space-sm`, whereas separate plant care cards leverage `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids harsh artificial shadows and stark industrial lines, relying instead on **canopy-soft ambient depth** and **tonal layering**.

### Elevation Stack
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** `#F7F9F6`. Soft sage-tinted canvas. Ground layer for background scrolling.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Modular Modules):** `#FFFFFF` layered over base with an ultra-diffused, foliage-tinted shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(30, 75, 56, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(35, 42, 38, 0.03);`
   - Border: 1px solid `rgba(30, 75, 56, 0.06)` for gentle definition without visual hardness.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Floating Care Triggers):**
   - `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 75, 56, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(35, 42, 38, 0.06);`
   - Applied to bottom care action sheets, water spray floating buttons, and diagnostic camera selectors.
4. **Level 3 (Modals & Full Bottom Drawers):**
   - High-diffusion backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) accompanied by a subtle semi-transparent overlay (`rgba(35, 42, 38, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The shape philosophy mirrors the curves of leaves, flower petals, and polished river stones. A balanced roundedness scale (`roundedness: 2`) ensures tactile touch targets on hand-held devices.

### Structural Radius Application
- **Base Surfaces & Cards:** `1rem` (16px) corner radius creates a friendly, non-threatening frame for plant imagery and health logs.
- **Pills & Status Chips:** Full round (`9999px`) for task statuses ("Cần tưới", "Đã bón phân", "Ánh sáng tốt").
- **Thumbnails & Botanical Media:** `0.75rem` (12px) soft clipping, preventing harsh corners from clashing with natural plant silhouettes.
- **Input Fields & Search Bars:** `0.75rem` to `1rem` for effortless thumb placement.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button (Care Confirmation):** Solid Deep Forest Green (`#1E4B38`) with pure white text, 48px height minimum for thumb target accessibility, `rounded-lg` (16px). Active press scales to `0.98` with a momentary tint shift.
- **Secondary / Action Button (Snooze / Alternate Care):** Soft off-tint container (`#EEF3EC`) with Forest Green text and no strong border.
- **Terracotta Accent Button (Immediate Attention):** Solid Terracotta (`#D97746`) reserved for crucial actions like "Trị sâu bệnh" or "Bù nước khẩn cấp".

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Compact height (28px - 32px), pill-shaped (`9999px`).
  - *Hydrated/Optimal:* Light mint surface (`#E6F5ED`), Emerald text (`#3E9B72`).
  - *Dry/Thirsty:* Warm sand surface (`#F9EFE9`), Terracotta text (`#D97746`).
- **Filter Chips (Garden Categories):** Bordered with `rgba(30, 75, 56, 0.12)`, turning solid Forest Green when active.

### Plant Cards & Care Cards
- **Plant Profile Card:** Horizontal or vertical layout featuring a 1:1 rounded visual thumbnail, species title in `headline-md`, care streak badge, and mini progress bar showing hydration depletion.
- **Care Task Card:** Checkable row with a circular interactive target on the right. Shows an illustrative SVG badge for the task type: droplet (water), sun (light), shear (pruning), spade (re-potting).

### Lists & Activity Logs
- Dividers are styled using soft sage hairline rules (`rgba(30, 75, 56, 0.08)`), inset by 16px to maintain visual cohesion.
- Growth journal lists incorporate miniature polaroid-like photos with date-stamped micro typography (`label-sm`).

### Checkboxes & Care Toggles
- Custom circular check controls (24px diameter) resembling water droplets or organic seeds. 
- In unchecked state: 1.5px border in `#C8D7CD`. 
- When checked: fills with `#3E9B72` accompanied by a smooth, spring-damped micro-scale animation.

### Input Fields
- Subtle surface background (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px soft sage border (`rgba(30, 75, 56, 0.15)`).
- Clear, floating placeholder text in `#728077`.
- Focus state activates a 1.5px border in `#1E4B38` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(30, 75, 56, 0.1)`).

### Specialized MiniApp Components
- **Soil Moisture / Sun Ring:** Circular SVG gauge tracking real-time sensor parameters, rendered in `#3E9B72` with background track in `#EEF3EC`.
- **Bottom Navigation Dock:** Fixed bottom floating bar with frosted glass styling (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` + blur), holding icon-first navigation items: "Khu vườn", "Nhật ký", "Khám bệnh", "Cài đặt". Active states present an organic teardrop indicator above the icon.
